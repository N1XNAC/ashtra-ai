About Orbit

A calm way to look up

Orbit is an independent research studio building calm tools for looking up. We make dense sky data legible, private by default, and beautiful on any screen — whether you are checking what is overhead from a city balcony or logging a full night of observing from a dark-sky site.

Private by default

Your observation logs stay on your device unless you explicitly share them. No ad tracking, no selling data, no account required to start exploring.

Built by observers

Every feature starts as a need from our own star parties and field sessions. If it does not help someone see more, it does not ship.

Readable data

Catalogs, ephemerides, and satellite passes translated into plain language and clear visuals you can act on in seconds.

Features

Everything you need to observe

Live sky maps, guided observation sessions, and shareable logs of everything you see. Everything syncs across your devices, and no account is required to start.

Live sky map

A real-time map of what is above you right now — planets, bright stars, satellites, and the ISS pass overhead in about an hour.

Guided sessions

Step-by-step observing runs that adapt to your location, the season, and how much time you have tonight.

Observation logs

Record what you saw with timestamps, seeing conditions, and sketches. Export or share a link in one tap.

Alerts & reminders

Get notified before an ISS pass, an aurora window, or the night your target clears the horizon.

Offline mode

Download tonight's sky before you leave home — the whole catalog works with no signal in the field.

Cross-device sync

Start a session on your phone, finish it on a tablet. Everything stays in step automatically.
